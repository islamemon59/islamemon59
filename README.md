## 👋 About Me

I'm a **MERN Stack Developer** from Bangladesh who enjoys building full-stack web applications that are fast, secure, and user-friendly.

I work with **React**, **Node.js**, **MongoDB**, and **Firebase** to bring ideas from design to deployment. Right now I'm deep into Stripe payment integration, JWT-based auth flows, and building role-based dashboards for real-world platforms.

- 🔭 Currently building a **Parcel Delivery System** with dynamic pricing & Stripe
- 🌱 Learning advanced **MongoDB aggregation pipelines** and **Firebase + JWT auth**
- 💡 Passionate about clean architecture, developer experience, and impactful products
- 🎓 **BA in English**, Shanto-Mariam University of Creative Technology (2023–2026)
- 🌍 Languages: **Bangla** (native) · **English** (intermediate)
